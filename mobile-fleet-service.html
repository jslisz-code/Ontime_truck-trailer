MOBILE FLEET SERVICE

Fleet maintenance where your vehicles already are.

Routine maintenance, diagnostics and unexpected repairs delivered directly to your fleet location to help reduce downtime and unnecessary shop visits.

Mobile repair built around uptime.

On-Time’s mobile fleet service is designed to keep vehicles running without the downtime and hassle of traditional shop visits. Technicians come directly to the customer location with tools and equipment needed to perform service efficiently.

Fleet service capabilities

  • Routine and preventative maintenance
  • Vehicle diagnostics
  • Unexpected mechanical repairs
  • Truck and trailer service
  • Flexible fleet scheduling

Why mobile service matters

Keeping vehicles at the operating location can reduce transportation to and from a repair facility, limit driver disruption and help fleet managers schedule maintenance around the operation.
